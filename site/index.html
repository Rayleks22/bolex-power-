Who we are

Power you can count on — day and night

Bolex Power Solutions designs and installs solar and inverter systems that keep the lights on when the grid goes off. From a single-home backup to commercial and industrial installations, we do the load assessment first, size the system correctly, and use quality equipment so your backup actually lasts as promised.

Alongside solar we handle complete electrical wiring, CCTV and security systems, and smart home, audio-visual and lighting work — one trusted team for the whole property.

  • Free energy audit and load assessment before we quote
  • Lithium battery systems and quality brand equipment
  • Installation, maintenance, servicing and repairs
  • Delivered promptly once payment is made
