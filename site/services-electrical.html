Electrical Wiring & Industrial Power

Safe, standard-compliant wiring for homes and commercial buildings — plus industrial power, 3-phase systems and certified safety inspections.

What we do

Wiring done once, done right

Whether it is a new build, a tired old installation or a factory floor, our electricians deliver clean, safe and properly protected wiring — with the documentation to prove it.

  • New builds, rewiring and extensions — complete electrical first and second fix
  • Commercial wiring for shops, offices, plazas, hotels and schools
  • Fault finding and repair — we trace the problem, not just the symptom
  • Earthing, lightning and surge protection
  • Distribution boards and breakers — installation and upgrades
  • Electrical safety inspection with completion certificate
  • Industrial power and 3-phase systems

Who it's for

From single rooms to full factories

Residential

House wiring for new builds, complete rewiring of older homes, extensions and safe upgrades for growing families.

Commercial

Shops, offices, plazas, hotels and schools — wiring, distribution boards, lighting circuits and safety compliance.

Industrial

3-phase systems, factory wiring, control panels and heavy power distribution — backed by real project experience, including a completed 185 kWh installation.

Safety comes standard

Every job includes proper earthing and circuit protection where required. For completed works we carry out an electrical safety inspection and issue a completion certificate — useful for insurance, handovers and peace of mind.

Buying or taking over a property? We also perform standalone electrical inspections so you know exactly what you are getting into before problems appear.

Also available

  • Surge protection for sensitive equipment
  • Lightning protection for exposed buildings
  • Distribution board upgrades and re-wiring of breaker panels
  • Fault tracing for tripping circuits and burning sockets
