Solar & Inverter Installation

From 5 kW home systems to 100 kW+ commercial and industrial installations — sized by load assessment, built with quality equipment.

What we do

Complete solar & inverter systems

We design, supply and install complete solar and inverter systems: panels, hybrid inverters, lithium batteries, charge controllers, cabling and protection — tested and handed over ready to use.

  • Systems from 5 kW up to 100 kW+ — homes to commercial and industrial scale
  • Lithium batteries for longer life and deeper usable capacity
  • Solar for street lights, water pumps and farms
  • Equipment supply as well as installation — buy what you need, installed properly
  • Maintenance, servicing and repairs for existing systems
  • Energy audit and load assessment to size the right system before you spend

The Bolex difference

Backup that lasts as promised

Anyone can mount panels on a roof. The difference is in the sizing. We calculate your real energy needs — fans, fridges, ACs, pumps, lighting — then design the inverter, battery and solar array around those numbers with a proper margin.

  • Load assessment before every quote
  • Right inverter capacity for your peak demand
  • Battery bank sized for your required backup hours
  • Solar array sized to recharge reliably, even in rainy season

Maintenance, servicing & repairs

Already have a solar or inverter system? We service, diagnose and repair systems of any brand — battery health checks, inverter faults, panel cleaning and wiring issues. We also offer equipment supply only if you prefer to install yourself.

Equipment supply

Inverters, solar panels, lithium batteries, charge controllers, cables and accessories — genuine products from the brands we install and supply, delivered to your site.
